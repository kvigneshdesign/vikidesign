I’m a Senior UI/UX Designer with 6.5+ years of experience across print, mobile, and web design. I craft accessible, user-centric digital experiences by combining strong visual design, problem-solving, and modern design tools.
With a background in fine arts, design is my craft—not just my job. I love solving complex problems and creating intuitive, engaging experiences that help businesses succeed and leave a lasting impression.
